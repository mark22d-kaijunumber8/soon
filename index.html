Soon

A countdown app about enjoying the wait, not just measuring it.

Every countdown gets a small living scene that moves closer to the big day as the time passes. A night flight glides toward the city lights, a sailboat crosses the sea to a lighthouse, a candle burns down on a frosty windowsill. On the day itself, each one celebrates in its own way.

What's inside

  • 24 looks: journeys and still lifes, each with its own celebration.
  • The wait in sleeps, weekends, full moons, movies or your own weekly ritual.
  • A time capsule that stays sealed until zero.
  • Surprises for future you, hidden behind the days ahead.
  • Widgets, and a live timer on the Lock Screen in the final 24 hours.
  • Memories afterwards, and yearly events that roll over on their own.

Soon is free for iPhone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and everything you add stays on your phone.
