Soon Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Soon is a countdown app for iPhone, made by Lun Do. The short version: Soon doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you add to the app is sent anywhere.

What stays on your iPhone

Your countdowns and everything in them (names, dates, notes, photos, voice notes, surprises and the name you give the app) are stored only on your iPhone. Soon's widgets and Live Activities read them from there too. Nobody else can see them, including us.

Like the rest of your apps, Soon's data is part of your iPhone's backups, to iCloud or to a computer, if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy. Deleting Soon deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used for the reminders Soon sends, like the evening before a countdown and at the moment itself. They're scheduled on your phone.
  • Microphone: used only while you record a voice note for a time capsule. The recording is saved on your phone.
  • Photos: you pick photos with Apple's photo picker, and Soon only gets the ones you choose. It can't see the rest of your library.
  • Motion: some scenes move when you tilt your phone, like the hourglass. The motion is read while the scene is on screen, and it's never stored.

Children

Soon collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version of Soon works differently, for example by letting you share a countdown with friends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
