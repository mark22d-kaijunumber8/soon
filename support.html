Support

Something not working, or an idea for Soon? Email notch.app.support@gmail.com and you'll get a reply.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Soon. To pick which countdown it shows, touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget. If you don't pick one, it shows the next one coming up.

How do I change a countdown's look?

Open the countdown, then press and hold its scene. Swipe through the looks, each drawn for your countdown as it is today, and tap the one you want.

Where's the timer on my Lock Screen?

In a countdown's last 24 hours, open Soon once and a live timer appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. iOS only lets it start while the app is open, and ends it after 8 hours, so Soon starts it again the next time you open the app. To turn it off for one countdown, open it, tap the pencil and switch off Show on Lock Screen.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on for Soon in the Settings app, under Notifications. Soon reminds you at 7pm the evening before, and at the moment itself, or at 8am for a countdown without a time.

How do I delete a countdown?

Swipe its card to the left in the list, or touch and hold it. You can also open it, tap the pencil and choose Delete countdown.

Will I lose my countdowns if I get a new phone?

Soon keeps everything on your iPhone, and it's included in your iPhone's backups. Restore your new phone from a backup and your countdowns come with it. Deleting the app deletes them.
